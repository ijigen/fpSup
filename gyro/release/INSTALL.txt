fpGyroSup v1.1 — SIGMA fp firmware Ver.5.02 only

Copy AutoRun.txt, VSHL.BIN, PGEN.BIN and the GYRO folder together to the ROOT
of an SD card. Do not mix them with files from v1, debug builds or test builds.
Put the card in the camera, power on, and wait for the display to reach fpSup!
before recording.

Each completed CinemaDNG take produces:

    \GYRO\A001_017.GYR                 raw six-axis capture
    \CINEMA\A001_017\A001_017.gcsv     Gyroflow IMU log
    \CINEMA\A001_017\A001_017.json     Gyroflow lens profile

After recording, fpGyroSup keeps the camera's native saving state active while
it closes GYR and writes GCSV then JSON. A new recording is locked, and normal
power-off waits, until the work completes. This took about 9–11 seconds for the
42–51 second validation clips. Do not pull the battery during this period.

CinemaDNG on SD is verified. MOV receives a GYR but no GCSV/JSON in v1.1.
External SSD recording has not yet been tested.

Nothing is flashed. This is RAM injection. Remove the three root files or pull
the battery to restore the camera. Forced interruption of a sidecar is not
repaired on the next boot.

fpGyroSup v1.1 —— 僅適用 SIGMA fp 韌體 Ver.5.02

請把 AutoRun.txt、VSHL.BIN、PGEN.BIN 與 GYRO 資料夾當成同一組，整批複製到
SD 卡根目錄。不要混用 v1、debug 版或測試版檔案。卡片放進相機開機，等畫面顯示
fpSup! 後再開始錄影。

每段完成的 CinemaDNG 都會產生上列三個檔案。

停止錄影後，fpGyroSup 會維持相機原生保存狀態，依序完成 GYR 關檔、GCSV 與
JSON。在此期間下一段錄影會被鎖住，正常關機也會等待。正式驗證的 42～51 秒素材
約需 9～11 秒完成；請勿在這段期間拔電池。

目前已驗證 SD 卡的 CinemaDNG。MOV 在 v1.1 只有 GYR，沒有 GCSV／JSON；外接
SSD 尚未測試。

本程式只做 RAM 注入，不會刷寫韌體。移除根目錄的三個檔或拔電池即可復原。
若強制中斷 sidecar 寫入，下次開機不會自動補寫。
