fpGyroSup v1.2 — SIGMA fp firmware Ver.5.02 only

Copy AutoRun.txt, VSHL.BIN and PGEN.BIN together to the ROOT of an SD card.
Do not mix them with files from v1, v1.1, debug builds or test builds.
Put the card in the camera, power on, and wait for the display to reach fpSup!
before recording.

Each CinemaDNG take produces, while it is being recorded:

    \CINEMA\A001_017\A001_017.gcsv     Gyroflow IMU log (1250 Hz gyro, 50 Hz accel)
    \CINEMA\A001_017\A001_017.json     Gyroflow lens profile

There is no .GYR any more and nothing happens after you press stop: the GCSV
streams to the card during the take and the JSON is written a few seconds
after the take starts. Stop, power off, or start the next take at will.

Verified with CinemaDNG 1920x1080 29.97p on SD with a prime lens. MOV takes get
no sidecars in v1.2. External SSD, UHD and zoom lenses have not been tested.

Nothing is flashed. This is RAM injection. Remove the three root files or pull
the battery to restore the camera.

fpGyroSup v1.2 —— 僅適用 SIGMA fp 韌體 Ver.5.02

請把 AutoRun.txt、VSHL.BIN、PGEN.BIN 當成同一組,整批複製到 SD 卡根目錄。
不要混用 v1、v1.1、debug 版或測試版檔案。卡片放進相機開機,等畫面顯示 fpSup!
後再開始錄影。

每段 CinemaDNG 在錄影中就會直接產生上列兩個檔案。

v1.2 沒有 .GYR,按下停止後也沒有任何後續工作:GCSV 在錄影期間持續串流寫卡,
JSON 在開錄幾秒後就寫好。要停、要關機、要接著錄下一段都可以。

已驗證 SD 卡 CinemaDNG 1920x1080 29.97p 搭配定焦鏡。MOV 在 v1.2 不會產生
sidecar;外接 SSD、UHD、變焦鏡尚未測試。

本程式只做 RAM 注入,不會刷寫韌體。移除根目錄的三個檔或拔電池即可復原。
