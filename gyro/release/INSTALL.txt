fpGyroSup v1.3 — SIGMA fp firmware Ver.5.02 only

Copy AutoRun.txt, VSHL.BIN and PGEN.BIN together to the ROOT of an SD card.
Do not mix them with files from v1, v1.1, v1.2, debug builds or test builds.
Put the card in the camera, power on, and wait for the display to reach fpSup!
before recording. That takes about nine seconds.

Each CinemaDNG take produces, while it is being recorded:

    \CINEMA\A001_017\A001_017.gcsv     Gyroflow IMU log (1250 Hz gyro, 50 Hz accel)
    \CINEMA\A001_017\A001_017.json     Gyroflow lens profile

There is no .GYR and nothing happens after you press stop.

Holding the camera upright works too. A portrait take is stored without the
rotation tag, so Gyroflow reads it the way it reads a landscape one: load the
sequence and both sidecars, sync, stabilise, and turn the picture ninety
degrees at the end of the edit. Leave horizon lock off. Photographs still
record their orientation and still rotate by themselves.

Verified with CinemaDNG 1920x1080 29.97p on SD with a prime lens, landscape and
portrait, takes to two and a half minutes. MOV takes get no sidecars. External
SSD, UHD and zoom lenses have not been tested.

Nothing is flashed. This is RAM injection. Remove the three root files or pull
the battery to restore the camera.

fpGyroSup v1.3 —— 僅適用 SIGMA fp 韌體 Ver.5.02

請把 AutoRun.txt、VSHL.BIN、PGEN.BIN 當成同一組,整批複製到 SD 卡根目錄。
不要混用 v1、v1.1、v1.2、debug 版或測試版檔案。卡片放進相機開機,等畫面顯示
fpSup! 後再開始錄影,大約九秒。

每段 CinemaDNG 在錄影中就會直接產生上列兩個檔案。沒有 .GYR,按下停止後也沒有
任何後續工作。

直拿也可以。直拿的片段不會寫入旋轉標籤,所以 Gyroflow 會像處理橫拿素材一樣讀它:
載入序列與兩個 sidecar、同步、穩定,最後在剪輯時把畫面轉九十度。鎖定水平請保持
關閉。照片仍然會記錄方向,自動旋轉不受影響。

已驗證 SD 卡 CinemaDNG 1920x1080 29.97p 搭配定焦鏡,橫拿與直拿,最長兩分半。
MOV 不會產生 sidecar;外接 SSD、UHD、變焦鏡尚未測試。

本程式只做 RAM 注入,不會刷寫韌體。移除根目錄的三個檔或拔電池即可復原。
