How to use it怎麼用

This takes you from an empty SD card to a working camera. Steps 1 to 6 are the same for everyone. Step 7 depends on what you put on the card. Step 8 is how to remove it.

這會帶你從一張空白記憶卡走到一台能用的相機。 第 1 到 6 步對每個人都一樣,第 7 步看你放了什麼進卡裡,第 8 步是怎麼移除。

Steps 3 to 5 need a computer. You have to copy two files onto an SD card. A phone cannot do this. If you are reading on a phone, read to the end now, then come back when you have a computer and a card reader. The whole guide is on this one page. 第 3 到 5 步需要電腦。你得把兩個檔案複製到記憶卡上, 手機做不到。如果你現在用手機看,先把整篇讀完, 等你有電腦和讀卡機時再回來。整份指南都在這一頁上。

  1. Check your camera and firmware確認你的相機和韌體

    You need a SIGMA fp with firmware Ver.5.02.

    你需要一台SIGMA fp,韌體是 Ver.5.02。

    To check: MENU → SYSTEM → Information.

    怎麼看:MENU → SYSTEM → Information。

    Both things matter.
    The fp L is a different camera with a different sensor. Nothing here works on it.
    And Ver.5.02 is not a minimum. A card is built for the exact places things sit in Ver.5.02. On any other version it writes to the wrong places, and it can go wrong in bad ways. If your camera is not on Ver.5.02, stop here.
    兩件事都很重要。
    fp L 是另一台相機、另一顆感光元件,這裡的東西在它上面都不能用。
    還有,Ver.5.02 不是最低版本。卡片是照著 Ver.5.02 裡東西所在的確切位置做的, 在其他版本上它會寫到錯的地方,而且可能出很糟的錯。 如果你的相機不是 Ver.5.02,到這裡就停下來。

  2. Check your card is fast enough確認你的卡夠快

    Open gate and the gyro both write to the card while you record. A slow card is the most common reason something here does not work.

    Open gate 和陀螺儀在你錄影時都會寫卡。 卡太慢是這裡的東西不能用最常見的原因。

    Open gate needs a lot of speed:

    Open gate 需要的速度很高:

    what you record你錄什麼speed needed需要的速度
    Open gate 3K, 24p, 12-bitOpen gate 3K,24p,12-bit221 MB/s
    Open gate 3K, 24p, 10-bitOpen gate 3K,24p,10-bit184 MB/s
    Open gate 3K, 24p, 8-bitOpen gate 3K,24p,8-bit148 MB/s
    Open gate 2K, 24p, 12-bitOpen gate 2K,24p,12-bit98 MB/s
    Normal FHD, 12-bit一般 FHD,12-bit97 MB/s
    the UHS-II SD card we measured我們量過的 UHS-II SD 卡94 MB/s
    an external SSD we measured我們量過的外接 SSD390 MB/s

    If the card is too slow, the camera stops recording after a few seconds. This is the card, not a bug. No setting can fix it. We have 31 open gate 3K clips recorded to that 94 MB/s card and every one of them stops at 4.1 seconds or less — the camera fills its ~600–750 MB buffer at a 127 MB/s deficit and then has to stop.

    如果卡太慢,相機會在幾秒後停止錄影。 這是卡的問題,不是 bug,沒有任何設定救得了。
    我們在那張 94 MB/s 的卡上錄了 31 段 open gate 3K, 每一段都停在 4.1 秒以內 —— 相機以 127 MB/s 的赤字把大約 600–750 MB 的緩衝吃完,然後就只能停。

    Open gate 3K is not an SD card away. The gap is not a few percent like FHD — it is 2.4×. And it is worse than the table looks: CinemaDNG writes one file per frame, and one open gate 3K frame is 9.2 MB. On our card, writes that size run at 66 MB/s, not 94 — 94 is what it manages in 50 MB blocks, which is not how the camera writes. So the real gap is closer to 3×.

    Open gate 3K 不是換張卡就能解決的。 這個差距不像 FHD 那樣只差幾個百分點 —— 是 2.4 倍。
    而且比表上看起來更糟:CinemaDNG 是一格一個檔, 而一格 open gate 3K 是 9.2 MB。在我們那張卡上,這個大小的寫入只跑 66 MB/s,不是 94 —— 94 是它在 50 MB 大區塊下的成績,而相機不是那樣寫的。 所以真實差距更接近 3 倍。

    We have only measured one SD card, and we never found where its curve flattens, so we cannot tell you that no SD card can do this. What we can tell you is that nothing we have measured in an SD slot is close, and the only storage we have measured that is comfortably fast enough is an external SSD over USB-C. If you want to try a faster card, run the benchmark below first — the number printed on the card is not the number you get.

    我們只量過一張 SD 卡,而且沒有量到它的速度曲線在哪裡走平, 所以我們不能跟你說「沒有任何 SD 卡做得到」。
    我們能說的是:我們在 SD 插槽裡量過的東西沒有一個接近, 而唯一我們量過、而且餘裕充足的儲存,是 USB-C 外接 SSD。
    想試更快的卡的話,先跑下面那個 benchmark —— 卡上印的數字不是你拿到的數字。

    The storage benchmark measures your real speed on the camera. The number printed on the card is often higher than the speed you actually get.

    儲存 benchmark 會在相機上量出你的真實速度。卡上印的數字,通常比你實際拿到的高。

    If you want to stay on SD, use OG2K. It needs 98 MB/s, which is essentially the camera's own FHD rate (97). So the test is simple: if your card records normal FHD cleanly, it records OG2K. It shows the same full-sensor 3:2 picture and uses well under half the data of OG3K.

    Be aware that at that rate a 94 MB/s card is already running flat out — it works because the camera buffers, but that is exactly why gyro samples get dropped and why writes can stall for a few hundred milliseconds. Faster card, fewer of those problems.

    想繼續用 SD 卡的話,用 OG2K。它需要 98 MB/s, 基本上就是相機自己的 FHD 碼率(97)。所以判斷方式很簡單: 你的卡能乾淨錄一般 FHD,就能錄 OG2K。 它呈現的是同樣的全感光元件 3:2 畫面,資料量遠不到 OG3K 的一半。

    要知道的是,在這個碼率下,一張 94 MB/s 的卡已經是滿載 —— 它能動是因為相機有緩衝,但那正是陀螺儀樣本會掉、 以及寫入會卡住幾百毫秒的原因。卡越快,這些問題越少。

    The gyro needs a good card too. It writes its files while you record, so it shares the card with the video. If the card is struggling, the gyro files are the first thing to go missing. You still get the clip, but the motion files are missing or incomplete. If that happens, check the card before you blame the build.
    陀螺儀也需要一張好卡。它在你錄影的同時寫檔, 所以是跟影片共用同一張卡。
    如果卡在吃力,陀螺儀檔案會是第一個不見的東西。 片子你還是有,但運動檔會缺少或不完整。
    遇到這種情況,先檢查卡,再去怪版本。

  3. Choose what goes on the card選擇卡片上要放什麼

    Open fpSup-Merge and tick what you want. It all runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded and you install nothing.

    打開 fpSup-Merge,勾選你要的東西。 全部在你的瀏覽器裡執行,不會上傳任何東西,你也不用安裝任何東西。

    tick勾選what you get你會得到
    og3k or og2k The full 3:2 sensor, as a new resolution in the camera menu完整的 3:2 感光元件,以一個新解析度出現在相機選單裡
    gyro Gyroflow motion files, written next to your clip as you recordGyroflow 運動檔,在你錄影時寫在片子旁邊

    You will also see shell on the list. Leave it off. It is a tool for developers: it lets a computer talk to the camera over USB, and it adds nothing you can see or use on the camera itself. If you do want it, the setup is in the fp_usb_shell README.

    清單上你還會看到 shell,不要勾它。 那是給開發者用的工具:它讓電腦透過 USB 跟相機對話, 在相機本身上看不到也用不到任何東西。
    如果你真的想要,設定方式在 fp_usb_shell 的 README。

    Everything you tick goes on one card and works together.

    你勾的所有東西會放進同一張卡,而且可以一起運作。

    This is the only place that can combine them. Do not try to combine two releases by copying both sets of files yourself. That card will not start.

    這是唯一能把它們合併起來的地方。 不要自己把兩個發行版的檔案都複製過去想手動合併 —— 那張卡開不起來。

    One rule: OG2K and OG3K cannot go on the same card. The camera has three resolution slots and each one needs the third slot. Pick one. The page will not let you tick both.
    一條規則:OG2K 和 OG3K 不能放在同一張卡上。 相機有三個解析度欄位,而它們兩個都需要第三個欄位。選一個就好 —— 那個頁面也不會讓你兩個都勾。

  4. Download the two files下載那兩個檔案

    You get AutoRun.txt and fpSup.BIN (a download published before 2026-09-19 names the second file VSHL.BIN; the two files always travel together, so use whichever pair you downloaded). That is the whole card. There is nothing to unzip and nothing to install. This is true no matter how many things you ticked.

    你會拿到 AutoRun.txt 和 fpSup.BIN (2026-09-19 之前發布的下載,第二個檔案叫 VSHL.BIN; 這兩個檔案永遠是一組的,所以你下載到哪一組就用哪一組)。
    那就是整張卡的全部內容。沒有東西要解壓縮,也沒有東西要安裝 —— 不管你勾了幾項都一樣。

    If you changed the banner text, use plain English letters and numbers only. The camera's screen font cannot show anything else.

    如果你改了開機橫幅的文字,只能用英文字母和數字。 相機螢幕的字型顯示不出其他東西。

  5. Copy the files to the card把檔案複製到卡上

    Turn the camera off and take the battery out. Then take the card out and put it in a card reader.

    關機,然後把電池拿出來。接著把卡拿出來,插進讀卡機。

    Copy both files to the top level of the card:

    把兩個檔案都複製到卡的最上層:

    correct正確will not work不會動
    /AutoRun.txt
    /fpSup.BIN    		 /fpSup/AutoRun.txt
    /DCIM/AutoRun.txt

    Do not make a folder. Do not rename anything. Do not convert anything.

    不要建資料夾。不要改名字。不要轉換任何格式。

    Also check the small lock switch on the side of the card is not locked. A locked card is the most common reason nothing happens in step 6.

    另外檢查卡片側邊那個小鎖扣,確認它沒有鎖上。 卡片被鎖住,是第 6 步什麼都沒發生最常見的原因。

  6. Put the card in and turn the camera on把卡插進去,開機

    A counter appears in the top left of the screen and fills up:

    螢幕左上角會出現一個計數器,然後慢慢填滿:

    fpSup[####....]050

    When it finishes, it is replaced by a banner naming what you loaded, ending with !. The name is whatever you set in fpSup-Merge — for example fpSup! or fpSup-OG3K-v0.2.3a!.

    跑完之後,它會被一行橫幅取代,寫著你載入了什麼,結尾是 !。 那個名字就是你在 fpSup-Merge 裡設定的 —— 例如 fpSup! 或 fpSup-OG3K-v0.2.3a!。

    Expect ten to twenty seconds, depending on how much is on the card.

    大概十到二十秒,看卡上放了多少東西。

    The banner means it worked. If you see it, go on to step 7. If you do not see it, go to the problems list instead of trying again.

    看到橫幅就表示成功了。看到的話,繼續做第 7 步。 沒看到的話,不要再試一次,去看問題清單。

    This happens every time you turn the camera on, as long as the file is on the card. Nothing is installed. The camera reads the card and builds it all again in memory each time. That is why removing it is easy.

    只要那個檔案還在卡上,每次開機都會這樣。 沒有任何東西被安裝。相機每次都重新讀卡、在記憶體裡把整套東西再建一次 —— 這就是為什麼要移除很容易。

  7. Use what you loaded使用你載入的東西

    This step depends on what you ticked in step 3. If you ticked both, they both work at the same time.

    這一步要看你在第 3 步勾了什麼。兩個都勾的話,兩個會同時生效。

    Open gate — OG3K or OG2KOpen gate — OG3K 或 OG2K

    First, turn Super35 / crop off. OG3K cannot record while crop is on. You press record and nothing happens, and the camera does not tell you why. Check this before you blame the card.

    先把 Super35 / crop 關掉。裁切開著的時候 OG3K 錄不了。 你按下錄影會什麼都沒發生,而相機不會告訴你為什麼。 在怪卡片之前,先檢查這個。

    Then choose the new resolution in the camera's normal menu: MENU → recording → resolution, or Quick Set → RES. Choose the frame rate and CinemaDNG quality as you normally would.

    然後在相機一般的選單裡選那個新解析度: MENU → recording → resolution,或 Quick Set → RES。
    格率和 CinemaDNG 品質就照你平常的方式選。

    This is a real menu item, so the camera remembers your choice. That is why step 8 has an order you need to follow.

    這是一個真正的選單項目,所以相機會記住你的選擇。 這就是為什麼第 8 步有一個你必須照著走的順序。

    Gyro陀螺儀

    There is nothing to turn on and no menu to find. While you record, it writes two files inside the clip's own folder:

    沒有東西要開,也沒有選單要找。 你錄影的時候,它會在片子自己的資料夾裡寫兩個檔案:

    A001_037.gcsvthe motion log運動記錄
    A001_037.jsonthe lens profile鏡頭 profile
    CinemaDNG only. A MOV take gets no files. MOV records through a different path that this build does not touch.
    只有 CinemaDNG。用 MOV 拍不會有這些檔案。 MOV 走的是另一條路徑,這個版本沒有碰到那裡。

    In Gyroflow, load the frames and both files, then run the sync.

    在 Gyroflow 裡載入影格和那兩個檔案,然後執行同步。

    You cannot skip the sync, and you cannot reuse a number from last time. CinemaDNG has no timecode, and the log starts about half a second after the first frame — between 470 and 570 ms, and different in every take.

    同步不能跳過,也不能沿用上次的數字。 CinemaDNG 沒有時間碼,而記錄的起點比第一個影格晚大約半秒 —— 落在 470 到 570 ms 之間,而且每一次拍攝都不一樣。

    Gyroflow asks for a frame rate when you give it a DNG sequence. Enter the rate you recorded at — it cannot read that from the frames.

    你把 DNG 序列丟給 Gyroflow 時,它會問你格率。 填你錄影時用的那個 —— 它沒辦法從影格裡讀出來。

    If you work in DaVinci Resolve如果你用 DaVinci Resolve

    There is a Gyroflow plugin for Resolve, so you can stabilise on the timeline instead of exporting from Gyroflow first. The two sidecar files are the same ones; the plugin reads them.

    Resolve 有 Gyroflow 的外掛,所以你可以直接在時間軸上穩定, 不用先從 Gyroflow 匯出。
    那兩個 sidecar 檔案是同樣的東西,外掛讀得懂。

    If you use an adapter or a manual lens如果你用轉接環或手動鏡頭

    The .json is the lens profile. The camera makes it from its own calibration data for the lens that is mounted, so it needs a lens with electronic contacts.

    .json 是鏡頭 profile。 相機是用它自己對「目前掛上的那顆鏡頭」的校正資料做出來的, 所以需要一顆有電子接點的鏡頭。

    With a manual lens, or through an adapter, the camera may not know the lens or may report the wrong one. The .json is then wrong, and Gyroflow will correct the picture in a way that does not match your real lens. The motion log (.gcsv) is not affected — that part is always correct.

    用手動鏡頭、或透過轉接環時,相機可能不認得那顆鏡頭、 或是回報成錯的那一顆。那樣 .json 就是錯的, Gyroflow 會用一組跟你真實鏡頭對不上的參數去修正畫面。
    運動記錄(.gcsv)不受影響 —— 那部分永遠是對的。

    What to do: do not use the .json. Use Gyroflow's own lens calibration instead. It is built in: you film a calibration target, and Gyroflow works out the real distortion of your lens and saves a profile you can reuse for that lens.

    怎麼辦:不要用那個 .json, 改用 Gyroflow 自己的鏡頭校正。
    它本來就內建:你拍一張校正圖卡,Gyroflow 會算出你鏡頭真正的畸變, 存成一個 profile,以後那顆鏡頭都能重複用。

    Portrait takes need nothing special — the camera records every frame landscape upright, so Gyroflow reads them like any other take. Leave horizon lock off.

    直拍的素材不用做任何特別處理 —— 相機每一格都是以橫向正立記錄的,所以 Gyroflow 讀它跟讀其他素材一樣。 水平鎖定維持關閉就好。

    Some builds write a single raw .GYR file instead of these two. If that is what you have, the converter turns it into the same pair, in your browser.

    有些版本寫出來的是一個原始的 .GYR 檔, 而不是這兩個。如果你拿到的是那個, 轉換器會在你的瀏覽器裡把它變成同樣的那一對。

    Test before you shoot something important. Everything here is still being tested. Every release has a README.txt that says what was tested and what was not. Read the one for what you loaded. Then record something you do not need, and check it, before you record something you do.
    拍重要的東西之前,先測試。 這裡的一切都還在測試中。每個發行版都有一個 README.txt, 寫明哪些測過、哪些沒測 —— 讀你載入的那一個。
    然後先錄一段你不需要的東西、檢查它,再去錄你真正需要的。

  8. Removing it — put your settings back first移除 — 先把設定改回去

    The order matters. This is the step people get wrong.
    The camera remembers menu settings on its own. Deleting the files does not clear them. So if you leave the camera set to an fpSup resolution and then delete the files, the camera is still set to something that is no longer there. It will not record properly.
    順序很重要。這就是大家會做錯的那一步。
    相機會自己記住選單設定,刪掉檔案不會把它清掉。
    所以如果你把相機停在 fpSup 的解析度上、然後才刪檔案, 相機就還停在一個已經不存在的東西上,那樣錄不出正常的東西。

    First, while the card still works:

    第一步,趁卡片還能運作的時候:

    Set the resolution back to a normal one — UHD or FHD — in MENU → recording → resolution or Quick Set → RES. If you changed any other fpSup menu item, set that back too.

    在 MENU → recording → resolution 或 Quick Set → RES 裡,把解析度設回正常的 —— UHD 或 FHD。
    如果你還改過其他 fpSup 的選單項目,也一起設回去。

    Then:

    然後:

    Delete AutoRun.txt from the card. Turn the camera off. Take the battery out. Put it back and turn the camera on.

    把卡上的 AutoRun.txt 刪掉。關機。 把電池拿出來。裝回去,再開機。

    Your camera is now completely normal. Nothing was written to the camera itself, so there is nothing else to undo.

    你的相機現在完全恢復正常。 相機本體上什麼都沒有被寫入,所以也沒有別的東西要還原。

    Take the battery out, do not just restart. A warm restart has not been tested.

    要把電池拿出來,不要只是重開。熱重開沒有測試過。

    Did you already do it in the wrong order? Nothing is broken. Put the card back in with its AutoRun.txt, turn the camera on so the resolution exists again, set it back to a normal one, and then delete the file. The setting is only a problem while the code behind it is missing.
    已經照錯的順序做了?沒有東西壞掉。
    把帶著 AutoRun.txt 的卡插回去、開機讓那個解析度重新存在、 設回正常的、然後再刪檔案。
    那個設定只有在背後的程式碼不見時才會是問題。

what happens發生什麼what to do怎麼辦
