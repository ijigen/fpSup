CAMERA VALIDATION BOUNDARY

Passed with the full UI debug form on SIGMA fp Ver.5.02:

  - CINE Settings collapsed OG3K summary and three-row list
  - Quick Set large/small OG3K values, cursor, and three-choice footer
  - FHD recording transition: 1 pass
  - UHD recording transition: 3 passes, about 1 second each
  - OG3K recording transition: 3 passes, about 1 second each
  - UI remained responsive and OG3K assets were rechecked afterwards

One earlier OG3K attempt froze after creating a clip. It was not reproduced by
the later three one-second attempts. The test SD card measured about 94 MB/s and
cannot sustain UHD or OG3K30 (~273 MB/s), so these results do not validate
duration. Playback with the v0.2 UI runtime, sustained recording on fast media,
inactive screen/style variants, and complete end-to-end coverage of all eight
frame rates remain untested.

The exact no-shell AutoRun/VSHL pair in this release has not yet received its
own camera cold-boot run. Its product payload is structurally identical to the
tested debug form, but this is still a v0.2.0test boundary, not a final release.

RAM ONLY / REMOVAL

All destinations are in RAM. Nothing writes flash. Delete or rename AutoRun.txt,
power off completely, REMOVE THE BATTERY, then power on to return to stock.

Only AutoRun.txt and VSHL.BIN belong on the SD card. OG3KUI.BIN, OG3KUI.NBR,
generated tables, debug captures, readbacks and A/B packages are build/test
material and are intentionally not shipped here.
