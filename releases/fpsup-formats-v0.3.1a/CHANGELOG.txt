v0.3.1a  2026-10-01   replaces v0.3.0a
================================================================

  Same six rows and UI as v0.3.0a.  Three fixes, all measured on
  the camera with the clips analysed on a computer:

  Fixed

    - OG3.5K: first frame of ~70 % of takes with an extra top line
      and swapped Bayer rows (magenta).  Window 6062x4040 ->
      6062x4032 so the ISP's /1.75 gives 2304 lines exactly; raster
      3464x2304, DNG crop 3456x2304 (was 2308).  23 of 23 clean.
    - S16: first frame of every take was old buffer content (white
      after a cold boot, another format's frame after a switch, the
      previous take otherwise).  Window 2112x1248 -> 2112x1250 (even
      crop margin) and a hook that skips the recorder's first DMA
      address replacement of each S16 take, which came 2 ms before
      S16's first active line.  DNG crop still 2096x1238.  5 of 5
      clean in the cases that always failed.
    - Playback header "W x H" for S16, OG2K and OG3.5K: the five
      missing number images are on the card.
    - S16: the focus magnifier (4x and 8x) keeps the S16 crop in the
      background instead of showing the whole frame.

  Changed

    - Menu text "OG3.5K 3456x2304".  Source: the published v0.3.0a
      now rebuilds byte for byte from the sigmafp-mods repository
      (build.sh checks it), and so does this build.

  Not verified

    - S16 first-frame fix on a 30-take series (5 measured).
    - Everything listed under v0.3.0a that was not verified then.

================================================================
v0.3.0a  2026-09-28   replaces v0.1.0a (0.2.x were internal)
================================================================

  Contains

    Six rows in the Resolution menu: UHD, FHD (stock), S16, OG2K,
    OG3K, OG3.5K.  Native UI in Settings (list, closed row) and
    Quick Set (tile, six-label footer with cursor).  12-bit
    CinemaDNG on every row.  Loader and stage2 of 2026-09-25.

  Added

    - S16: Super 16 crop, 2112x1248 photosites at 1:1 (12.55 x
      7.41 mm), DNG crop 2096x1238, 23.98..29.97, 95 MB/s at 24p;
      standby live view cropped and centred.
    - OG3.5K: whole 3:2 sensor read 1:1 and scaled /1.75 by the ISP
      (window 6062x4040), raster 3464x2308, DNG crop 3456x2308,
      23.98..29.97, 290 MB/s at 24p 12-bit; live view through the
      stock UHD video profile (hardware-binned, fluid, no reframe
      when REC starts).
    - Frame rates a row cannot record are greyed (popup and Quick
      Set); the stored rate is kept.  OG2K up to 100, OG3K up to
      59.94, 119.88 greyed on both.
    - Crop Mode greyed and effectively OFF on the new rows, stored
      value kept.
    - Cold boot: the saved row is applied by the card ~20 s after
      loading, display refreshed.
    - Switching rows inside Quick Set updates the live view (with a
      one-frame crop flash when the aspect changes).

  Changed

    - Built through fpSup's shared loader (power-off write-back of
      every changed firmware word); no USB shell on the card.
    - Combines on fpSup-Merge with Gyro / Gyro-Base / Shell / Fast
      Start 2 (page checks); not with OG3K or OG2K cards.
    - Registry: the UHD 12-bit video profile is a donor (its raster
      copy in k49/k50 was the missing piece); OG3K keeps its stock
      profile values; two spare registry slots stay empty.

  Removed

    - FHD+, 3K+, OG2K+, OG3K+ (OG3.5K supersedes OG3K+; the /3 rows
      aliased; 3K+ was SSD-only for no gain over OG3K).
    - The need to turn Super35 / crop OFF by hand before selecting a
      new row.

  Not verified (alpha)

    8/10-bit on S16 and OG3.5K, in-camera playback of the new rows,
    MOV or stills with a new row selected, the combined cards on the
    camera.  Media-speed table (Samsung T5) in README.txt.

v0.2.0a .. v0.2.1a  2026-09-27   internal
================================================================

  Eight-row set (UHD, FHD, FHD+, 3K+, OG2K, OG2K+, OG3K, OG3K+) on
  the shared loader; frame-rate greying, effective values, crop
  greying, cold-boot apply.  Used for the feasibility study that
  led to v0.3.0a (OG4K rejected: its preview freezes the display;
  OG3.5K and S16 measured).

================================================================
v0.1.0a  2026-09-24   withdrawn -- use v0.3.0a
================================================================

  First card with the eight-row Resolution menu: the format registry
  (six formats x eight frame rates), full-photosite "+" rows scaled
  by the ISP, native eight-row UI.  Recording measured on every row.
  Known then, fixed since: frame rates above 29.97 offered on the
  "+" rows and silently recorded at 29.97; Super35 / crop had to be
  OFF by hand; after a battery pull the saved row was not applied
  until re-selected; loader of 2026-09-22 (patches stayed in memory
  across a warm restart); development USB shell on the card.
