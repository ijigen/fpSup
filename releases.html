Release notes and older cards版本說明與舊版卡片

The latest card version of each product, including earlier card formats. Each release's notes explain its layout, tested behaviour and limits.

各產品最新的卡片版本,也包含沿用舊卡片格式的產品。 每個版本的說明列出卡片結構、測試結果與限制。

To choose tools for a new card, use fpSup-Merge. Its recording formats are combined in Res-Custom. Older single-container cards are made in the previous fpSup-Merge; follow the guide for the card you use.

製作新卡片請到 fpSup-Merge 選擇功能; 目前的錄影格式整合在 Res-Custom。 舊的單一容器卡片使用舊版 fpSup-Merge製作, 請依照所用卡片的指南操作。

fpsup-gyrov1.14.1test Writes Gyroflow .gcsv motion and .json lens files while recording CinemaDNG. This test build needs on-camera audio sync validation.錄製 CinemaDNG 時寫出 Gyroflow 的 .gcsv 運動紀錄與 .json 鏡頭檔。此測試版的聲畫同步仍待實機驗證。
fpsup-gyro-basev1.14.0 Writes the gyro and accelerometer as a raw .GYR in the root of the disk the take went to — every sample, nothing on the camera but the stream — converted afterwards in a browser. Same code as fpGyroSup v1.14.0.在錄影那顆磁碟的根目錄寫一個原始 .GYR(陀螺儀與加速度計每個樣本都在),機上只做串流,事後在瀏覽器轉換。與 fpGyroSup v1.14.0 同一份程式。
fpsup-gyro2v0.1.0 Writes the fp's gyro and level data into every CinemaDNG frame; the Gyroflow (fpSup) plugin for DaVinci Resolve reads it from the frames and stabilises the clip, with no sync step. Works with Lossless.把 fp 的陀螺儀與水平儀資料寫進每一格 CinemaDNG;DaVinci Resolve 的 Gyroflow (fpSup) 外掛直接從畫面檔讀取並防震，不用手動同步。可與 Lossless 同卡。
fpsup-losslessv0.2.0test Lossless-compressed CinemaDNG, by the camera's own hardware codec: a Lossless RAW row (SHOOT page 2, CINE) turns it on; frames the codec cannot finish in time are written uncompressed, and compressed clips play back in the camera. Test build.用相機自己的硬體編碼器錄無損壓縮 CinemaDNG:SHOOT 第 2 頁(CINE)多一列 Lossless RAW 開關;來不及壓的畫格照原樣寫入未壓縮,壓縮過的片段可在機內回放。測試版。
fpsup-og2kv0.1.5a The same open gate at 2016×1344, on the sensor's quiet readout at every frame rate. 98 MB/s at 24p 12-bit, 8.3 ms rolling shutter. Test build.同樣的 open gate,2016×1344,每個幀率都走感光元件的安靜讀出。24p 12-bit 為 98 MB/s,捲簾 8.3 ms。測試版。
fpsup-og3kv0.2.8a The sensor's whole 3:2 area at 3024×2010, eight frame rates from 23.976 to 100, native UI in Settings and Quick Set. 221 MB/s at 24p 12-bit — this needs an external SSD, not an SD card. Alpha.感光元件完整的 3:2 面積,3024×2010,八個幀率從 23.976 到 100,Settings 與 QS 有原生 UI。24p 12-bit 為 221 MB/s ——這個碼率要外接 SSD,SD 卡不夠。Alpha。
fpsup-raw-viewv0.3.0test RAW monitoring for CinemaDNG 12-bit: a RAW row in the COLOR menu makes the LCD show what will be recorded — recording gain in standby, sensor saturation as white, two latitude curves (SA/GA) mapped onto a 709 screen. The recorded RAW is not changed. Loader v3 card; settings are kept in its own file, not in the camera. Test build.CinemaDNG 12-bit 的 RAW 監看:COLOR 選單多一列 RAW,螢幕顯示即將錄下的內容——待機用錄影增益、感光元件飽和即白、兩條寬容度曲線(SA/GA)映射到 709 螢幕。錄下的 RAW 不被改動。Loader v3 卡;設定存在自己的檔案,不寫相機設定。測試版。
fpsup-res-customv0.1.2test Open Gate formats in one: OG2K, OG3K, OG3.5K, OG4K and S16 as extra movie resolutions in Settings and Quick Set, each a small data file in fpSup\RESCUS; the camera's own tables are not changed. Test.Open Gate 各格式合一:OG2K、OG3K、OG3.5K、OG4K、S16 成為 Settings 與 QS 的錄影解析度新選項,每個格式是 fpSup\RESCUS 裡的一個小資料檔;不改相機原廠的任何表。測試版。
fpsup-screenflipv0.1.0test Flip the rear screen 180°, mirror it, or both — set separately for each custom display mode, applied when you switch into that mode. Test build.背螢幕翻轉 180°、鏡像或兩者 —— 每個自訂顯示模式各自設定,切換進那個模式時才套用。測試版。
