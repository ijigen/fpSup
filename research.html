The products are the part of this that fits on an SD card. Most of the work is reading disassembly, and every project page keeps three things apart: what has been proven on a camera, what is being worked on, and what is still open.

Helping想幫忙的話

The most useful thing anyone can contribute is a measurement: a card's sustained write rate, a frame rate nobody has tested, footage from a body other than the one everything here was developed on. The Discord is where that gets coordinated, the repository has all of it, and there is a YouTube channel.

任何人能提供的東西裡,最有用的是一筆量測:一張卡的持續寫入速度、 一個沒人試過的格率、或是來自另一台機身的素材 —— 這裡的一切都只在同一台機器上開發出來。 協調這些事的地方在 Discord, 全部的東西都在程式庫裡, 另外還有一個 YouTube 頻道。
