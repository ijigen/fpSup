Firmware research

The products are the part of this that fits on an SD card. Most of the work is reading disassembly, and every project page keeps three things apart: what has been proven on a camera, what is being worked on, and what is still open.

Explainers

IMX410 ISO and gain Where the sensor's gain is actually applied, what that does to dynamic range at each ISO, and which parts are confirmed in the firmware versus which OTP values still need measuring. Interactive. read this one first · the most finished piece of research here From sensor to CinemaDNG The whole recording chain in eleven stages — menu, picker, the 70 sensor modes, the 306 profiles, the reduction stages, the canvas and the DNG writer — each one expandable, with what OG3K, OG2K and HSW each change and an honest mark on which parts are solved, partial or still dark. the map · read this before the two below Every setting on the sensor The 161 values the fp writes to the IMX410, one row each — 150 of them now decoded, without a datasheet, by grouping registers on which modes share a value. Merge select, bit depth and the readout-speed block are identified; 11 are still open. Plus the eight ROM tables the values live in and all 70 modes they combine into. reference · sortable, filterable, read from Ver.5.02 Hbin, Vbin, Hbin2 and RWZM The fp has two unrelated ways to shrink a frame and six names for where to tap the result — and only two of those six are ever switched on. The decision that picks between them, recovered from the firmware and run over all 306 recording profiles. Interactive. new · corrects three claims in the older notes In-camera lossless compression The fp compresses every still it takes and has never compressed a video frame. The engine's measured cost model, where it sits in the pipeline — after every reduction, so you cannot resize afterwards — and a simulator for frame rate, dynamic frame-skip and how much the picture happens to compress. interactive budget · FHD fits, UHD does not The firmware shell The fp carries a factory debug shell with 77 commands, and a text file on the SD card runs them at boot with no permission gate — which is what everything else here is built on. All 77 with the firmware's own usage text, searchable, and the three tiers of how much damage each one can do. reference · 54 of 77 asked on a live camera On-screen text colours The colour of display text is a palette index, not a colour value, and the stock pink is an out-of-range one. All 256 indices, with the thirty that come from ROM kept apart from the ones that had to be photographed. reference · 0–29 exact, 161–255 measured

Stopped or paused

Kept because what they established still holds.

bridge The most complete thing built on the fp's PTP surface. Its measurements — why host-side autofocus is hard, and that UHD 12-bit CinemaDNG cannot come over USB — are load-bearing here
gimbal SIGMA's 0x94xx vendor protocol works during recording, unlike tethered focus. Relative drive only, but absolute position is readable
color sup The colour is reproduced; the tool is awkward and nothing is packaged
fpRemote Not started. Wireless bridge and low-resolution streaming through AutoRun rather than PTP

Helping

The most useful thing anyone can contribute is a measurement: a card's sustained write rate, a frame rate nobody has tested, footage from a body other than the one everything here was developed on. The Discord is where that gets coordinated, and the repository has all of it.
