fpSup-RAW-View

Adds a RAW row to the COLOR menu. With it selected and the camera set to CinemaDNG 12-bit, the screen shows the RAW the camera is about to record, not a finished picture. The recorded RAW is not changed. Version v0.3.0test — a test build.
Display curves after Ole Berek’s Rec709 LUT guide; the CinemaDNG colour tags corrected with Luka.

在 COLOR 選單加上一列 RAW。選到它、而且錄影格式是 CinemaDNG 12-bit 時, 螢幕顯示的是即將錄下的 RAW,而不是修飾過的畫面。錄下的 RAW 不會被改動。 版本 v0.3.0test —— 這是測試版。
顯示曲線依據 Ole Berek 的 Rec709 LUT 指南;CinemaDNG 色彩標籤的修正來自 Luka。

Thanks致謝

Thank you, Ole Berek感謝 Ole Berek

SIGMA fp Rec709 LUT & Operations Guide v3 · Ole Berek · 2023-06-28

RAW-View's display curves are built on this guide. Both curves use its latitude figures — how many stops the fp holds above and below 18% grey at each ISO (p. 2) — and the GA curve is its “LA - SIGMA Rec709” curve (p. 4), digitised. Without his careful work this view would have had to guess. Any mistake in how we applied it is ours, not the guide's.

RAW-View 的顯示曲線建立在這份指南上。兩條曲線都用它的寬容度數據 —— fp 在每個 ISO 於 18% 中灰上下各有幾檔(第 2 頁);GA 曲線則是它的 「LA - SIGMA Rec709」曲線(第 4 頁)數位化而來。沒有他細心的整理,這個畫面就只能用猜的。 我們套用時若有任何錯誤,責任在我們,不在這份指南。

Thanks致謝

Thank you, Luka感謝 Luka

Luka tested RAW-View on their own fp with control shots seconds apart and showed, frame by frame, that the RAW is not altered. Then Luka found what our own checks could not: at Saturation +0.2 the CinemaDNG colour tags described the camera's white balance as a different white, so Resolve showed the clip about 1100 K cooler than the same shot at 0. Luka worked out the fix, with tests built to fail on the old tags, and v0.2.4test carries it. Luka also shared a chart-fitted colour matrix and an ARRI-style view that we are still evaluating.

Luka 用自己的 fp 以相隔幾秒的對照拍攝測試 RAW-View,逐格證明 RAW 沒有被改動。 接著 Luka 找到我們自己的驗算看不到的問題:飽和度 +0.2 時,CinemaDNG 的色彩標籤把相機的白平衡描述成另一個白, Resolve 顯示的畫面比同場景在 0 時偏冷約 1100 K。Luka 推導出修正,連同專門會在舊標籤上失敗的測試, v0.2.4test 採用了它。Luka 也分享了色卡擬合的色彩矩陣與 ARRI 風格的畫面,我們還在評估。

Install安裝

Tick fpSup-RAW-View in fpSup-Merge, download, and follow the install steps on that page. Then set the recording format to CinemaDNG 12-bit.

在 fpSup-Merge 勾選 fpSup-RAW-View 下載, 照該頁的安裝說明放進卡。然後把錄影格式設為 CinemaDNG 12-bit。

Use使用

Press COLOR and pick row 17, RAW. In RAW:

按 COLOR,選第 17 列 RAW。在 RAW 下:

  • Standby uses the recording gain. What clips on the screen clips in the DNG, and what does not, does not.待機使用錄影時的增益。 螢幕上過曝的地方 DNG 也過曝,沒過曝的就沒有。
  • Sensor saturation is display white at every ISO.每個 ISO 的感光元件飽和都是顯示白。

The curve: SA or GA曲線:SA 或 GA

The box on the right of the RAW row shows SA or GA. Change it to change the curve. Both use the latitude figures of SIGMA fp Rec709 LUT & Operations Guide v3 (Ole Berek).

RAW 列右側的框顯示 SA 或 GA,改它就是換曲線。 兩者都用 SIGMA fp Rec709 LUT & Operations Guide v3(Ole Berek)的寬容度數據。

SA Latitude, Stop Aligned. The fp's latitude at the current ISO (stops above and below 18% grey) spread over the screen with Rec.709's own stop spacing.Latitude — Stop Aligned。 fp 在目前 ISO 的寬容度(中灰以上、以下幾檔),依 Rec.709 本身的檔距鋪在螢幕上。
GA Latitude, Gray Aligned. The “LA - SIGMA Rec709” curves of that guide.Latitude — Gray Aligned。那份指南的「LA - SIGMA Rec709」曲線。

AEL: Contrast and SaturationAEL:對比度與飽和度

Press AEL. In RAW only two settings are used, each 0 or +0.2. Sharpness is held at 0.

按 AEL。RAW 下只用兩項,各為 0 或 +0.2。清晰度固定為 0。

setting設定screen螢幕recorded CinemaDNG錄下的 CinemaDNG
Contrast 0對比度 0 All 12.5 stops fit on the screen. Live view resolves only about 10.9 stops below clipping, so the bottom ~1.6 stops show as fine noise dots: dots mean there is detail, pure black means crushed.12.5 檔全部放進螢幕。預覽在削波以下只分得出約 10.9 檔, 所以最底約 1.6 檔以細小噪點出現:有點就是有細節,純黑才是壓死。 Not affected不受影響
Contrast +0.2對比度 +0.2 Only the 10.9 stops live view resolves. The bottom ~1.6 stops go black and the rest get more room.只放預覽分得出的 10.9 檔。最底約 1.6 檔變黑,其餘檔位更寬。 Not affected不受影響
Saturation 0 (default)飽和度 0(預設) No colour calibration on screen.螢幕不套色彩校正。 Exactly the same as a normal recording與一般錄下的檔案完全相同
Saturation +0.2飽和度 +0.2 The camera's own calibration matrix (the one the other colour modes use) is applied.套用相機自己的校色矩陣(其他色彩模式用的那一組)。 The same matrix is written into the ColorMatrix tags, so Resolve shows the colours you monitored, at the same white balance as at 0. The raw pixels are not changed.同一組寫進 ColorMatrix 標籤, Resolve 顯示的顏色與監看一致,白平衡與 0 時相同。RAW 像素本身不動。

What is remembered記住了什麼

RAW is remembered across a power-off, together with SA/GA, Contrast and Saturation. Start the camera with the card in and it comes back in RAW as you left it.

關機後會記住 RAW,連同 SA/GA、對比度、飽和度。插著卡開機,就會回到上次的 RAW 設定。

  • It is saved at a normal power-off only. After a battery pull the last change is lost, as with the camera's own settings.只在正常關機時存檔。拔電池會失去最後一次的改動, 和相機自己的設定一樣。
  • It is not saved while a custom mode (C1–C3) is active.使用自訂模式(C1–C3)時不會存。

Things that behave differently行為不同的地方

  • Choosing OFF in Quick Set does not leave RAW. Choose OFF (or another mode) in the COLOR menu.在 Quick Set 選 OFF 不會離開 RAW。請在 COLOR 選單選 OFF 或其他模式。
  • In scenes with bright highlights, auto exposure can meter up to 1–2 EV darker than stock. Live view now has the highlight room 12-bit recording has.場景有亮部時, 自動曝光可能比原廠暗 1~2 EV。即時影像現在有 12-bit 錄影同樣的高光空間。

Remove移除

Put COLOR back first. RAW is saved as a colour mode the stock firmware does not know. If you remove the card while RAW is selected, the camera starts showing Standard, and the COLOR button menu can then change Standard's saved adjustments.
先把 COLOR 改回去。RAW 是以原廠不認得的色彩模式存檔。 如果選著 RAW 就把卡拿掉,相機開機會顯示 Standard,這時打開 COLOR 按鈕選單可能改到 Standard 存的調整值。

  1. While the card still works趁卡還能運作時

    Press COLOR and pick any stock colour mode (OFF, Standard, …).

    按 COLOR,選任一個原廠色彩模式(OFF、Standard……)。

  2. Then remove it然後移除

    Delete the files (or take the card out) and switch the camera off. The card writes back every firmware word it changed as the camera powers off. If the camera froze, take the battery out (USB unplugged). More on removing.

    刪掉檔案(或拔卡),然後關機。卡片改過的每個韌體字會在關機時寫回。 如果相機凍結了,把電池拿出來(不插 USB 線)。移除的詳細說明。

Already removed it while RAW was on? Nothing is broken. Start the camera without the card and pick any colour mode in the COLOR menu to clear it, or put the card back and do it in order. Until then, avoid the COLOR button menu while it shows Standard.
已經在 RAW 開著時移除了?沒有東西壞掉。不插卡開機,在 COLOR 選單選任一個色彩模式即可清掉; 或把卡插回去,照順序再做一次。在那之前,顯示 Standard 的期間請避免打開 COLOR 按鈕選單。
