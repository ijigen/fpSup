Open Gate formatsOpen Gate 各格式

Five extra CinemaDNG resolutions — OG2K, OG3K, OG3.5K, OG4K and S16 — chosen in Settings and Quick Set next to UHD and FHD, all from one card. Version v0.1.2test. This is a test release: treat every take as disposable until you have checked it.

五個額外的 CinemaDNG 解析度 —— OG2K、OG3K、OG3.5K、OG4K、S16 —— 在 Settings 與 Quick Set 裡和 UHD、FHD 並排選,全部在同一張卡上。版本 v0.1.2test。
這是測試版:每段錄影在你檢查過之前,都當成可能不能用。

Thanks致謝

Thank you, Vitaly Li and Jose Hurtado感謝 Vitaly Li 與 Jose Hurtado

Vitaly Li got open gate out of a SIGMA fp first (FP3K) — his work showed the fp could do it at all. S16, OG3.5K and OG4K are Jose Hurtado's formats (fpSup-Formats v0.6.1): his timings, readout windows and canvas values are carried here unchanged, as data.

Jose's work also shaped the core that runs every format here:

  • One core, one row of data per format. Res-Custom's layout follows his format registry, including how switching works: put every format's changes back to stock, then apply the chosen one.
  • Greying out what a format cannot do. He found where the camera decides which frame rates and whether Crop Mode can be picked, and that S16 needs the 8× magnifier. Res-Custom hooks the same places.
  • The video clamp. He found that the open-gate formats borrow stills modes whose black level steps from frame to frame at high ISO (flickering shadows), and fixed it by turning on the video clamp. Res-Custom does the same for all five formats.
  • Firmware facts read from his cards: how to read the current resolution and Crop Mode, and how the live-view frame and the Crop variants are set per format.
  • Camera tests of S16, OG3.5K and OG4K, recorded on his camera.

The code is ours, written from source; the ideas above and the three formats' numbers are his.

Visit Jose's blog, El Cacharrista. His code is on GitHub: github.com/jahurtado.

在 SIGMA fp 上第一個做出 open gate 的是 Vitaly Li(FP3K)—— 是他證明了 fp 做得到。 S16、OG3.5K、OG4K 是 Jose Hurtado 的格式(fpSup-Formats v0.6.1): 他的時序、讀出視窗與畫布數值原封不動,以資料的形式帶過來。

執行所有格式的核心,也有很多地方來自 Jose 的研究:

  • 一個核心、每個格式一列資料。Res-Custom 的架構照他的格式登錄表設計,連切換方式也一樣: 先把所有格式改過的地方寫回原廠,再套用選中的那一個。
  • 格式做不到的選項會反灰。相機在哪裡決定可選的格率、Crop Mode 能不能選,是他找到的; S16 要用 8 倍放大鏡也是他發現的。Res-Custom 掛在同樣的地方。
  • Video clamp。他發現 open gate 格式借用的靜態模式,在高 ISO 時黑位會逐格跳一階(暗部閃爍), 並以打開 video clamp 修正。Res-Custom 五個格式都照這樣做。
  • 從他的卡讀出的韌體知識:怎麼讀目前的解析度與 Crop Mode,以及每個格式的即時預覽框與 Crop 版本怎麼設定。
  • 實機測試:S16、OG3.5K、OG4K 在他的相機上錄過。

程式碼是我們從原始碼寫的;上面這些構想與三個格式的數值是他的。

歡迎逛逛 Jose 的部落格 El Cacharrista。他的 GitHub:github.com/jahurtado。

Install安裝

Checking the camera, copying the card and starting it: see the install steps on fpSup-Merge. What is particular to these formats:

確認相機、複製到卡、開機:見 fpSup-Merge 的安裝步驟。這幾個格式特有的部分:

  1. Pick the formats in fpSup-Merge在 fpSup-Merge 勾選格式

    Open fpSup-Merge with the Open Gate formats ticked. Every format is ticked by default; untick the ones you do not want (at least one must stay). You cannot add the older OG3K or OG2K cards, Jose's Formats card, or res-lab: they use the same parts of the camera. Download the zip.

    打開已勾好 Open Gate 各格式的 fpSup-Merge。 每個格式預設都勾著;不要的取消勾選(至少要留一個)。
    不能同時加舊的 OG3K / OG2K 卡、Jose 的 Formats 卡、res-lab:它們用的是相機的同一些地方。下載 zip。

  2. Keep the RESCUS folder別漏了 RESCUS 資料夾

    The formats live in fpSup/RESCUS/, one .RCD file each. Copy the whole fpSup folder with it. When you update the card, follow the install steps: an unticked format left on the card still loads.

    各格式在 fpSup/RESCUS/ 裡，每個格式一個 .RCD 檔。複製整個 fpSup 資料夾時要連它一起。更新卡時照安裝步驟做：沒勾的格式留在卡上仍會載入。

Storage speed. The UHS-II SD card we measured writes 94 MB/s; an external SSD 390 MB/s. OG3K, OG3.5K and OG4K need the external SSD. OG2K and S16 sit at the edge of an SD card: if it records normal FHD cleanly, try it. Too slow storage makes the camera stop recording after a few seconds.

Use使用

1. Set the recording format to CinemaDNG.

1. 錄影格式設成 CinemaDNG。

2. Choose the format in MENU → recording → resolution or Quick Set → RES, then the frame rate and bit depth as usual.

2. 選格式:MENU → recording → resolution 或 Quick Set → RES, 格率與位元深度照平常選。

Frame rates a format does not have are greyed out while it is chosen; your saved frame rate is kept and comes back with another resolution.

格式沒有的格率在選著它時會反灰;你存的格率會保留,換回別的解析度時回來。

Crop Mode: OG3.5K and OG4K follow it — Crop Mode on records the centre of the sensor 1:1 at the same DNG size. For OG2K, OG3K and S16 it is greyed out and off while they are chosen.

Crop Mode:OG3.5K 與 OG4K 跟著它 —— 開著時錄感光元件中央 1:1,DNG 尺寸相同。 OG2K、OG3K、S16 被選時它會反灰並關閉。

What is tested and what is not測過什麼、沒測過什麼

Tested on a SIGMA fp, Ver.5.02 (earlier builds of this card):

在 SIGMA fp、Ver.5.02 上測過(這張卡較早的建置):

  • Loading, the Settings list and Quick Set with all five formats.載入、Settings 清單與 Quick Set:五個格式全裝。
  • Recording OG3K 12-bit, S16 12-bit (with its standby framing), OG4K and OG3.5K 8-bit: correct DNG sizes, clean pictures.錄 OG3K 12-bit、S16 12-bit(含待機取景框)、OG4K 與 OG3.5K 8-bit:DNG 尺寸正確、畫面乾淨。
  • MOV with a format chosen records normal MOV.選著格式時錄 MOV,錄出正常的 MOV。

Not yet tested on the camera:

還沒在相機上測:

  • Crop Mode with OG3.5K / OG4K; the greyed frame rates and Crop Mode.OG3.5K / OG4K 的 Crop Mode;反灰的格率與 Crop Mode。
  • OG2K, OG3K and S16 at 100p (needs the SSD).OG2K、OG3K、S16 的 100p(要 SSD)。
  • OG4K standby framing; a format coming back after a restart.OG4K 待機取景框;重開機後格式自動回來。

Remove — or drop one format移除 —— 或拿掉一個格式

Drop one format: delete its .RCD from fpSup/RESCUS. If it was the format you had chosen, the camera starts on UHD or FHD.

拿掉一個格式:把它的 .RCD 從 fpSup/RESCUS 刪掉。 若那正是你選著的格式,相機開機後會是 UHD 或 FHD。

Remove everything: choose UHD or FHD first, then delete 32RESCUS.BIN and the RESCUS folder. Did it the other way round? Nothing is broken: the camera only ever stored UHD or FHD.

全部移除:先選回 UHD 或 FHD,再刪 32RESCUS.BIN 與 RESCUS 資料夾。 順序反了?沒有東西壞掉:相機本來就只存 UHD 或 FHD。
