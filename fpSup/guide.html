How to use it怎麼用

This page takes you from an empty SD card to a loaded camera, and back. It is the same for every product. What to do once it is loaded is on each product's own page.

這一頁帶你從一張空白記憶卡走到一台載入好的相機,再回到原狀。 這些步驟每個產品都一樣。載入之後怎麼用,在每個產品自己的頁面上。

product產品what it does做什麼
Res-Custom Custom recording resolutions in one sup: open gate OG2K, OG3K, OG3.5K, OG4K and S16.多種自訂錄影解析度合一:Open Gate OG2K、OG3K、OG3.5K、OG4K 與 S16。
Lossless Lossless-compressed CinemaDNG, by the camera's own hardware codec.用相機內建的硬體編碼器錄無損壓縮 CinemaDNG。
Gyro2 Gyro data inside every CinemaDNG frame; stabilise in DaVinci Resolve with the Gyroflow plugin (fpSup build).把陀螺儀資料寫進每一格 CinemaDNG,用 Gyroflow 的 DaVinci Resolve 插件(fpSup 版)防震。
RAW-View The screen shows the RAW about to be recorded: what clips on screen clips in the DNG.螢幕顯示即將錄下的 RAW:畫面上過曝的地方，錄下來就是過曝。
LUT Converts a .cube LUT for the camera's own colour hardware: on the screen, in JPG and in MOV (RAW stays RAW).把 .cube LUT 轉成相機自己的色彩硬體設定：螢幕、JPG 與 MOV 都套用（RAW 不變）。
ScreenFlip Flip the rear screen 180°, mirror it, or both.背螢幕翻轉 180°、左右鏡像，或兩者。

Older-format cards — OG3K, OG2K, Gyro, Gyro-Base — are still made on the previous fpSup-Merge.

舊格式的卡 —— OG3K、OG2K、 Gyro、Gyro-Base —— 仍在 舊版 fpSup-Merge 製作。

Install安裝

  1. Check your camera and firmware確認你的相機和韌體

    You need a SIGMA fp with firmware Ver.5.02.

    你需要一台SIGMA fp,韌體是 Ver.5.02。

    To check: MENU → SYSTEM → Information.

    怎麼看:MENU → SYSTEM → Information。

    Both things matter.
    The fp L is a different camera with a different sensor. Nothing here works on it.
    And Ver.5.02 is not a minimum. A card is built for the exact places things sit in Ver.5.02. On any other version it writes to the wrong places, and it can go wrong in bad ways. If your camera is not on Ver.5.02, stop here.
    兩件事都很重要。
    fp L 是另一台相機、另一顆感光元件,這裡的東西在它上面都不能用。
    還有,Ver.5.02 不是最低版本。卡片是照著 Ver.5.02 裡東西所在的確切位置做的, 在其他版本上它會寫到錯的地方,而且可能出很糟的錯。 如果你的相機不是 Ver.5.02,到這裡就停下來。

  2. Make the card in fpSup-Merge在 fpSup-Merge 做卡

    Open fpSup-Merge, tick what you want and download the zip. It all runs in your browser: nothing is uploaded and you install nothing. How to put it on the card is written once, on that page, under Install.

    打開 fpSup-Merge,勾你要的，下載 zip。 全部在你的瀏覽器裡執行,不會上傳任何東西，你也不用安裝任何東西。 怎麼放進卡，只寫在那一頁的安裝說明。

    A card is a folder: AutoRun.txt and a fpSup folder with one file per product, at the top level of the SD card. Everything you tick works together. Products that cannot run together both go on the card, and the page says which one is switched off.

    一張卡就是一個資料夾:AutoRun.txt 加上 fpSup 資料夾， 每個產品一個檔案，放在 SD 卡最上層。你勾的東西會一起運作; 不能同時運作的兩個也都會放進卡，頁面會告訴你哪一個是關著的。

    Check that the small lock switch on the side of the card is not locked. A locked card is the most common reason nothing happens at start-up.

    檢查卡片側邊那個小鎖扣，確認它沒有鎖上。 卡片被鎖住，是開機時什麼都沒發生最常見的原因。

    OG3K, OG2K, Gyro and Gyro-Base are older-format cards, still made on the previous fpSup-Merge. A card holds one format or the other, not both.

    OG3K、OG2K、Gyro、Gyro-Base 是舊格式的卡，仍在 舊版 fpSup-Merge 製作。一張卡只能是其中一種格式，不能混用。

  3. Put the card in and turn the camera on把卡插進去，開機

    The fpSup logo appears in the top left and four boxes fill one by one. All four white means it is loaded. The camera's own screen comes back two seconds later. fpSup\LOAD.LOG on the card records whether each product loaded.

    左上角會出現 fpSup 標誌,四個方格一個一個填滿。四個都變白就表示載入完成， 兩秒後相機自己的畫面會回來。卡上的 fpSup\LOAD.LOG 會記錄每個產品有沒有載入。

    Boxes that stop filling mean the load stopped there. Go to the problems list instead of trying again.

    方格停在一半,表示載入停在那裡。 不要再試一次,去看問題清單。

    This happens every time you turn the camera on, as long as the files are on the card. The camera reads the card and builds it all again in memory each time.

    只要檔案還在卡上,每次開機都會這樣。 相機每次都重新讀卡、在記憶體裡把整套東西再建一次。

Before you record: the card's speed錄影之前:卡的速度

Open gate and the gyro write to the card while you record. A slow card is the most common reason something here does not work. Each product's page lists the speed it needs. The number printed on a card is often higher than what the camera gets; the storage benchmark measures the real one on the camera.

Open gate 和陀螺儀在你錄影時都會寫卡。 卡太慢是這裡的東西不能用最常見的原因。每個產品頁都列了它需要的速度。 卡上印的數字常常比相機實際拿到的高; 儲存 benchmark 會在相機上量出真實速度。

Use what you loaded使用你載入的東西

Each product works differently: a new resolution in the menu, files next to your clip, or a new row in the COLOR menu. Open its page from the table at the top. If you ticked several, they all work at the same time.

每個產品的用法不一樣:選單裡多一個解析度、片子旁邊多出檔案、或是 COLOR 選單多一列。 從最上面的表打開它的頁面。勾了好幾個的話,它們會同時生效。

Test before you shoot something important. Everything here is still being tested. Each product's page says what was tested and what was not. Record something you do not need, and check it, before you record something you do.
拍重要的東西之前,先測試。 這裡的一切都還在測試中。每個產品頁都寫明哪些測過、哪些沒測。 先錄一段你不需要的東西、檢查它,再去錄你真正需要的。

Removing it移除

First put back any menu setting the card added. The camera remembers menu settings on its own, and deleting the files does not clear them. If the camera is left on an open-gate resolution, or on a row the card added, it is set to something that is no longer there. Each product's page says what to set back.
先把卡片加進來的選單設定改回去。 相機會自己記住選單設定,刪掉檔案不會把它清掉。 如果相機停在 open gate 的解析度、或是卡片加的那一列上, 它就停在一個已經不存在的東西上。每個產品頁都寫了要改回什麼。

Then: delete the files from the card, or take the card out, and switch the camera off. As it powers off, the card writes back every part of the camera's memory it changed, so the camera is back to normal. Nothing was written to the camera's flash, unless an older-format card had Fast Start 3 on.

然後:把卡上的檔案刪掉,或是把卡拿出來,然後關機。 相機關機時,卡片會把它改過的每一處記憶體寫回原狀,所以相機就恢復正常了。 除非舊格式的卡開了 Fast Start 3,否則相機的 flash 裡沒有被寫入任何東西。

Did you already do it in the wrong order? Nothing is broken. Put the card back with its files, turn the camera on so the setting exists again, set it back to a normal one, and then remove the files. The setting is only a problem while the code behind it is missing.
已經照錯的順序做了?沒有東西壞掉。 把帶著檔案的卡插回去、開機讓那個設定重新存在、改回正常的、然後再移除檔案。 那個設定只有在背後的程式碼不見時才會是問題。

If something does not work如果有東西不能用

These apply to every card. Problems with one product — no new resolution, no motion files, a strange picture — are in the table on that product's page.

這些適用於每一張卡。某個產品自己的問題 —— 沒有新解析度、沒有運動檔、畫面怪怪的 —— 在那個產品頁的表裡。

Nothing appears on the screen螢幕上什麼都沒出現 The card did not run. Check the file is called AutoRun.txt and is at the top level of the card. Check the lock switch on the card is off.卡片沒有執行。確認檔案名稱是 AutoRun.txt、而且在卡的最上層。 另外確認卡片上的鎖扣是關的。
The logo appears but no boxes出現標誌,但沒有方格 The fpSup folder (with UI inside it) is missing or not at the top level. Copy the whole folder from the zip.fpSup 資料夾(裡面有 UI)不見了，或不在最上層。從 zip 裡把整個資料夾複製過去。
The boxes stop filling方格停在一半 The load stopped there. Download the card again instead of reusing it. Tell us on Discord how many boxes filled.載入停在那裡。 重新下載一張卡,不要沿用原本那張。到 Discord 告訴我們填到第幾格。
It loaded but nothing changed載入了，但什麼都沒變 Check where you are looking: each product changes a different place. Its page says where. fpSup\LOAD.LOG says whether the product loaded; a file ending .OFF is switched off.先確認你在看哪裡: 每個產品改的地方不一樣，它的頁面有寫在哪裡。fpSup\LOAD.LOG 會寫產品有沒有載入; 副檔名是 .OFF 的是關著的。
Recording stops after a few seconds錄影幾秒後就停了 The card is too slow. See above, and the speed on the product's page.卡太慢。見上面, 以及產品頁上需要的速度。

The camera does not respond at all相機完全沒反應 Unplug the USB cable, then take the battery out. The next start is clean.先拔掉 USB 線,再把電池拿出來。 下一次開機就是乾淨的。

Removing it, in short: set back any menu setting the card added first (the product's page says which). Then delete the files or take the card out, and switch the camera off.

移除的濃縮版:先把卡片加進來的選單設定改回去 (產品頁有寫是哪個)。然後刪掉檔案或拿出卡片,再關機。
