fpSup

Firmware research and on-camera tools for the SIGMA fp. Everything here runs from AutoRun.txt on the SD card, in RAM only — remove the file, power-cycle, and the camera is stock. Nothing is written to flash. Firmware Ver.5.02 only.

Support on Ko-fi Discord

Tools

fpSup-Merge Pick the products you want on one card and get AutoRun.txt and VSHL.BIN. Merged cards are produced here and nowhere else; the page checks every combination against the same rules the build scripts use. runs in the browser · no toolchain

Each release is two files. Copy AutoRun.txt and VSHL.BIN to the root of the card — no folder to make, no step afterwards. To combine two of them, use the card composer rather than copying both.
