Will this work on my camera

Camera
SIGMA fp. Not the fp L. That is a different camera with a different sensor, and nothing here works on it.
Firmware
Ver.5.02 only. Not "5.02 or newer" — only this version. A card is built for the exact places things sit in Ver.5.02, and on any other version it writes to the wrong ones. Check under MENU → SYSTEM → Information.
Card
A fast card, for open gate and for the gyro. Open gate at 24p needs about 221 MB/s; a mid-range UHS-II card we measured does 94. If the card is too slow the camera stops recording, and gyro files go missing. A slow card is the most common problem people have.
Risk
Nothing is installed on the camera. It only lives in memory while the power is on, so there is no firmware to flash back. You remove it by deleting a file — but in the right order. The camera remembers menu settings on its own, so put those back to normal first. The guide shows you how.

What you can put on a card

productversionwhat it does
fpsup-gyrov1.12.1 Writes Gyroflow's .gcsv and .json while recording — every sample at 2500 Hz, distortion read off the lens. The profile now carries the size the take was actually recorded at, which open gate got wrong.
fpsup-og2kv0.1.1a The same open gate at 2016×1344, on the sensor's quiet readout at every frame rate. 98 MB/s at 24p 12-bit, 8.3 ms rolling shutter. Test build.
fpsup-og3kv0.2.4a The sensor's whole 3:2 area at 3024×2010, eight frame rates from 23.976 to 100, native UI in Settings and Quick Set. 219 MB/s at 24p 12-bit. Alpha.
fpsup-usbshellv3.1.1 The shell that answers shl over USB, parasitic on the camera's own PTP gadget so the firmware keeps owning the endpoints.

You do not download these one by one. You tick the ones you want in fpSup-Merge and it makes a single card with all of them on it. Click a name above to read that release's notes — what was tested, and what was not.

fpsup-usbshell is not for shooting. It is a development tool that lets a computer talk to the camera over USB. It adds nothing you can see or use on the camera. If you are here to record, you want og3k or og2k, and gyro.

Both open-gate builds show the same picture. OG2K is smaller: a third of the data, less rolling shutter (8.3 ms instead of 12.4), and the sensor's quiet readout at every frame rate including 100p. Pick OG2K if your card is not fast enough for OG3K. They cannot go on the same card. Both are still being tested. Open gate on an fp was first done by Vitaly Li.

To remove it: first set the resolution back to a normal one. The camera remembers your choice, and deleting the file does not clear it. Then delete AutoRun.txt, turn the camera off, take the battery out, and turn it on again. Full instructions.
