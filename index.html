fpSup

Firmware research and on-camera tools for the SIGMA fp. Everything here runs from AutoRun.txt on the SD card, in RAM only — remove the file, power-cycle, and the camera is stock. Nothing is written to flash. Firmware Ver.5.02 only.

Tools

Card composer Pick the products you want on one card and get AutoRun.txt and VSHL.BIN. Merged cards are produced here and nowhere else; the page checks every combination against the same rules the build scripts use. runs in the browser · no toolchain Storage benchmark A card that makes the camera measure its own SD and SSD write speed, using the firmware's own benchmark. Worth running before anything above FHD: CinemaDNG is limited by write speed long before anything else. no memory patches · not tied to a firmware version

Each release is two files. Copy AutoRun.txt and VSHL.BIN to the root of the card — no folder to make, no step afterwards. To combine two of them, use the card composer rather than copying both.
