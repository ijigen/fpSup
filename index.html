Firmware韌體
Ver.5.02 only — not “5.02 or newer”. The card writes to fixed addresses in Ver.5.02; on any other version those addresses are wrong. Check in MENU → SYSTEM → Information.
只限 Ver.5.02 —— 不是「5.02 或更新」。 卡片寫入的是 Ver.5.02 裡的固定位址,換了版本位址就是錯的。
在 MENU → SYSTEM → Information 確認。
Storage儲存
OG3K needs an external SSD. At 24p 12-bit it writes 221 MB/s (8-bit: 148). The UHS-II SD card we measured does 94, and only 66 with CinemaDNG's 9.2 MB per-frame writes — every OG3K clip on it stopped within 4.1 s. A USB-C SSD measured 390.
OG2K at 24p needs 98 MB/s, about the same as normal FHD 12-bit (97): if your card records FHD cleanly, it can record OG2K at 24p. Higher frame rates need more (60p: 244). A slow card is the most common problem.
OG3K 需要外接 SSD。24p 12-bit 要寫 221 MB/s(8-bit 是 148)。我們量過的 UHS-II SD 卡只有 94, 換成 CinemaDNG 每格 9.2 MB 的寫法只剩 66 —— 在它上面錄的每段 OG3K 都在 4.1 秒內停掉。USB-C 外接 SSD 量到 390。
OG2K 在 24p 要 98 MB/s,跟一般 FHD 12-bit(97)差不多: 卡能順利錄 FHD,就能錄 24p 的 OG2K。格率越高要越快(60p 要 244)。 卡太慢是最常見的問題。
Settings設定
Turn Super35 / crop off. Open gate cannot record while it is on.
關掉 Super35 / 裁切。開著的時候 open gate 錄不了。
Risk風險
Nothing is installed. It runs in memory only while the power is on; nothing is written to flash. To remove it, first set the resolution back to UHD or FHD (the camera remembers it), then delete AutoRun.txt and take the battery out. The guide shows how.
相機上什麼都不安裝。只在開機時跑在記憶體裡,不寫入 flash。 移除時先把解析度設回 UHD 或 FHD(相機會記住這個設定), 再刪掉 AutoRun.txt、拔電池。指南有步驟。

fpsup-usbshell is not for shooting. It is a developer tool that lets a computer talk to the camera over USB; it adds nothing you can use on the camera. Do not leave it on a card you record with. To record, pick og3k or og2k, plus gyro.

fpsup-usbshell 不是拍片用的。它是開發工具, 讓電腦透過 USB 跟相機溝通,相機上沒有任何可用的功能。 不要把它留在拍片用的卡上。
要錄影的話,選 og3k 或 og2k,再加 gyro。

OG3K and OG2K show the same full 3:2 view. OG2K records 2016×1344 instead of 3024×2010: a third of the data, a shorter rolling shutter (8.3 ms vs 12.4), and the sensor's quiet readout at every frame rate — OG3K's 100p uses the noisier fast readout. Pick OG2K if your card cannot keep up with OG3K. Only one of the two fits on a card. Both are still in testing. Open gate on the fp was first done by Vitaly Li.

OG3K 和 OG2K 的畫面範圍一樣,都是完整 3:2。OG2K 錄 2016×1344 而不是 3024×2010:資料量三分之一、捲簾更短(8.3 ms 對 12.4), 而且每個格率都走感光元件的安靜讀出 —— OG3K 的 100p 用的是雜訊較多的快速讀出。
卡跟不上 OG3K 就選 OG2K。兩者只能擇一放進同一張卡,都還在測試中。
在 fp 上第一個做出 open gate 的是 Vitaly Li。
