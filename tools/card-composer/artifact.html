Checks

One thing these checks do not cover. Section and entry checks cannot prove that hooks are safe before initialization finishes. Firmware background paths may reach a hook during loading, whether it belongs to a built-in or uploaded card. Validate that timing in the intended execution context; a correct file layout alone is not an on-camera safety test.

SHA-256

Firmware Ver.5.02 on the original fp only. Everything in the zip goes in the root of the SD card the camera boots from. Everything runs from RAM — nothing is ever written to flash. Switching the camera off is a warm restart that keeps the firmware in memory; the card writes every firmware word it changed back as the camera powers off. To get a stock camera back, remove the card and switch off; if the camera froze instead, take the battery out with the USB cable unplugged.
